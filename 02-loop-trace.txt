A trimmed trace of the live case. Each turn: think, act, observe, decide.
------------------------------------------------------------------------

  decide   Not done. Continue.

The decide step is the whole game. Without a stopping condition, an agent
keeps looping: it retries, re-reads, and spends tokens forever.
